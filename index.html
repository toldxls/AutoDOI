AutoDOI
Crossref and DataCite metadata, formatted for pasting

Paste a DOI, a doi.org link, or any text containing one. Copy gives plain text; Rich keeps italics for Word and Google Docs.

Paste a paper title and, if you have it, the journal name. The best matches come back with their DOIs; pick one to format it.

Paste one or more references in any style, one per line. Each is matched against Crossref, then exported as EndNote tagged (.enw) and RIS. Check the match colour before importing.

Settings
Article titles are shown as the publisher deposited them, so APA's sentence-case rule and MLA's title case may need a manual touch. Metadata comes live from api.crossref.org and doi.org; nothing is stored on a server.
