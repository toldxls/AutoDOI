AutoDOI

Crossref and DataCite metadata, formatted for pasting

Paste a DOI, a doi.org link, an arXiv ID, a PubMed ID, an ISBN, or any text containing one. Show every built-in style, pick one, or search 10,000 journal styles. Copy gives plain text; Rich keeps italics for Word and Google Docs.

Style Titles

Paste a paper title and, if you have it, the journal name. The best matches come back with their DOIs; pick one to format it.

 

Paste one reference or a whole list, in any style and however messy, and get it back clean: as a reference list in APA, Vancouver, Chicago or any of 10,000 journal styles, or as .enw, .ris or BibTeX for EndNote, Zotero and Mendeley. Each reference is matched against Crossref (with OpenAlex as a second opinion) and graded green, amber or red so you can see what to check; anything unmatched is kept exactly as you pasted it. You can also drop a .ris, .enw or .bib file, which is read directly with no lookup and re-exported in any style.
