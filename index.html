AutoDOI
Crossref and DataCite metadata, formatted for pasting

Paste a DOI, a doi.org link, an arXiv ID, a PubMed ID, or any text containing one. Show every built-in style, pick one, or search 10,000 journal styles. Copy gives plain text; Rich keeps italics for Word and Google Docs.

Paste a paper title and, if you have it, the journal name. The best matches come back with their DOIs; pick one to format it.

Paste one or more references in any style, one per line (or separated by blank lines if they wrap). Each is matched against Crossref, then exported as EndNote tagged (.enw), RIS, BibTeX, or a clean reference list in any style. Check the match colour before using.

Article titles are shown as the publisher deposited them, so APA's sentence-case rule and MLA's title case may need a manual touch. Metadata comes live from api.crossref.org and doi.org; nothing is stored on a server. Journal styles come from the Citation Style Language project (CC BY-SA 3.0) and are rendered in your browser by citeproc-js.
